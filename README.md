# Geared-Utility-Yoke-Underactuated-WCR

This project aims to develop a dual-motor magnetic wall-climbing robotic vehicle based on magnetic adhesion and underactuated mechanism design. The robotic vehicle employs an array of N42-grade NdFeB permanent magnets integrated onto a Q235 carbon structural steel frame, with precise adjustment of the magnetic circuit air gap to optimize the balance between adhesive force and motion resistance. At the drive and control level, the system adopts an encoder-based differential drive scheme implemented on an ESP32 microcontroller, enabling efficient coordination and precise motion control of the two motors. By combining gear transmission with underactuated manipulation design, the robotic vehicle achieves compactness and lightweight construction while delivering excellent performance and adaptability to ferromagnetic surfaces.
